## 2. About / Hotel Description

### Short Version (for hero section or meta description)
Villa Azur Djerba is an intimate 16-room boutique hotel sitting directly on Plage Sidi Mehrez, the jewel of Djerba Island's northeast coast. Renovated in 2020, it blends authentic Djerbian architecture — whitewashed walls, blue shutters, olive wood, and Berber craftsmanship — with modern comfort and heartfelt hospitality. Winner of TripAdvisor's Travelers' Choice Award 2025, Villa Azur is the antidote to the mass resort experience.

---

### Long Version (for About Us page)
Tucked behind a curtain of bougainvillea and jasmine on the shores of Sidi Mehrez, Villa Azur Djerba is not a hotel you stumble upon — it is one you seek out. A world away from the sprawling resort complexes of the island, our 16-room boutique retreat offers something rarer than a sea view: the feeling of being a welcomed guest, not a room number.

Built in the spirit of traditional Djerbian architecture, every corner of Villa Azur tells a story of place. Whitewashed facades echo the Mediterranean horizon. Blue shutters frame views of the sea. Inside, noble materials — hand-laid local marble, aged olive wood, Guellala ceramics from the island's own potters, and Berber rugs woven by artisan hands — create interiors that are warm, deeply personal, and unmistakably Tunisian.

Completely renovated in 2020, Villa Azur pairs this authentic aesthetic with everything a modern traveler deserves: a 500 m² thalassotherapy spa, a signature restaurant drawing on our organic kitchen garden, a rooftop cocktail bar at sunset, private beach dinners by the water's edge, and a level of personalized attention that is only possible at our scale.

We were awarded TripAdvisor's Travelers' Choice 2025 — an honor earned not by size, but by the consistency of care our guests experience every single day.

Whether you come to rest, to explore Djerba's remarkable cultural heritage, or simply to watch the sun melt into the Gulf of Gabès from your private balcony, Villa Azur is your home on the island.

## 5. Dining

### La Voile Restaurant

La Voile is Villa Azur's signature dining room — a space where Mediterranean tradition meets contemporary culinary creativity. Our head chef, trained in major European establishments, builds every menu around seasonal ingredients sourced from the hotel's own organic kitchen garden and trusted local suppliers across Djerba and southern Tunisia.

The menu changes with the seasons, but a few dishes have become beloved constants:

- **Seafood couscous revisited with Gafsa saffron** — a nod to Tunisia's most iconic staple, elevated
- **Lamb tajine with apricots and almonds**, slow-cooked for 7 hours over gentle heat
- **Fresh grilled catch of the day** with charmoula and preserved lemon

**Meal services:**
- **Breakfast:** Generous buffet with freshly squeezed orange juice, Tunisian pastries, cheese, cold cuts, yoghurts, and made-to-order crepes
- **Lunch:** Barbecue menus served in the garden under the pergola
- **Dinner:** À la carte Mediterranean fusion in the restaurant or on the terrace

---

### Le Palmier Bar

Climb to our rooftop bar as the afternoon turns golden. Le Palmier serves signature cocktails, curated wines, and Mediterranean tapas against a backdrop of panoramic sea views and Djerba Island's legendary sunsets over the Gulf of Gabès. The perfect way to close a day.

---

### Private Beach Dinner

Our most celebrated romantic experience. A candlelit table set directly on the sand of Sidi Mehrez beach, lanterns flickering around you, the sound of the sea as your soundtrack. Our team curates a custom menu tailored to the occasion. Available on request — advance booking recommended.

## 6. Spa & Wellness

### The Villa Azur Spa — 500 m²

A sanctuary for the body and mind, our thalassotherapy spa is one of the largest of its kind on the island — exceptional for a hotel of our intimate scale. Drawing on the therapeutic properties of Mediterranean seawater and centuries-old Tunisian care traditions, the spa offers a complete restorative journey.

**Facilities:**
- Heated seawater therapy pool
- Oriental hammam (traditional steam bath)
- Sauna
- Relaxation lounge

**Treatments & Services:**
| Treatment | Description |
|---|---|
| Thalassotherapy | Full-body seawater immersion and jet treatments |
| Black Soap Exfoliation (Gommage) | Traditional Tunisian full-body scrub with authentic savon beldi |

| Hot Stone Massage | Therapeutic massage using heated Djerba island stones with aromatherapy oils |
| Relaxation Massage | Gentle full-body massage with essential oils |

**Daily Classes (included for guests):**
- Morning yoga on the sun terrace (sea-facing)

All treatments available individually or as curated packages. Spa menu and booking available at reception.

## 7. Pool & Beach

### Outdoor Swimming Pool
Our sun-drenched outdoor pool is the heart of the hotel's social life — framed by tropical palms, flowering bougainvillea, and views across the garden to the sea. Sun loungers and parasols are reserved for hotel guests. Pool-side refreshments available from Le Palmier Bar.

### Private Beach — Plage Sidi Mehrez
Step through the garden gate and feel the sand of Sidi Mehrez beneath your feet. This is Djerba at its most beautiful: a long, quiet arc of fine sand on the island's northeast coast, sheltered from strong winds, with clear, warm, shallow waters. Beach umbrellas and loungers are provided complimentary for all guests.

## 8. Activities & Experiences

We believe a stay at Villa Azur should open a door into Djerba's rich culture, not just its beach. Our concierge team curates and arranges the following experiences for guests:

### On the Water
- Catamaran excursion to the **Kneiss Islands** — flamingo observation and snorkeling in protected marine waters
- Kayaking and paddleboarding from the hotel's beach
- Windsurfing and kitesurfing lessons (seasonal)

### Cultural & Heritage
- Guided tour of **Houmt Souk's medina**: souks, the jewellery quarter, and the historic Fondouk caravanserai
- Visit to **Bordj el Kebir** — 16th-century fortress overlooking the harbor
- Visit to **El Ghriba Synagogue** — one of the world's oldest houses of worship, pilgrimage site of exceptional spiritual significance
- **Taguermes Lighthouse** scenic walk
- **Djerba Explore Park** (Lalla Hadria Museum of Islamic Art + Crocodile Farm)

### Culinary & Artisan
- **Tunisian Cooking Class** led by Villa Azur's head chef — learn to prepare traditional dishes from scratch using market-fresh ingredients
- **Traditional Pottery Workshop** — hands-on introduction to Guellala ceramic techniques, the island's centuries-old craft tradition
- **Friday Midoun Market visit** — the island's most authentic weekly market, overflowing with dates, spices, local produce, and handmade crafts

### Nature & Adventure
- **Camel trekking** on Sidi Mehrez beach at sunrise or sunset
- **Quad biking excursions** across the island's interior
- **Olive farm visits** — experience Tunisia's ancient olive culture
